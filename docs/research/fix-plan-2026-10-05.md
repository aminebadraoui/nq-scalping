## F1 — the root fix: candle-synced live bars (code, deploy fast)

The live engine builds completed bars from the tick stream; the stream is
capturing ~7% of true volume, so every buy-frac/cluster/volume gate runs
on a 7% sample — the live cards (2W/8L on the archive tape) are a
different strategy than the backtested one.

- `lib/live.ts`: add a **rolling candle refresh** — every 60s fetch the
  completed minutes from the FOOTPRINT/V2 candle feed (the same feed the
  collector archives) and **overwrite** the tick-built bars (today's
  `seedFromFootprint` only fills gaps at bootstrap and never replaces).
  Mark each bar `source: "candle" | "tick"`.
- `lib/live.ts`: drop prints with no aggressor side instead of guessing
  up-tick = buy / down-tick = sell.
- `lib/signalFeed.ts`: a card may only fire on a candle-source bar;
  tick-source bars log + skip. (Signals fire on completed bars, so this
  costs nothing in the validated flow.)
- `/health`: expose the bar-source mix (candle % of the last 120 bars) —
  the 0.07 ratio becomes a monitored number instead of a silent killer.
- **Policy path**: data-fidelity fix — tag
  `[backtested: scripts/sizingOptimizer.ts --offline scripts/rthOptimizer.ts --offline]`
  (the detectors were validated on candle data; this commit makes live
  use that same data), full `npm test` + `npx tsc --noEmit`.
- **Effect**: live signals = archive signals; the entire validated WR/PF
  corpus applies again. Kills the tick-bar card population.
